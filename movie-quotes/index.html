Where'd You Go, Bernadette? (2019)

Bernadette Fox: "I used to design buildings. For a while, it was everything to me."

Bee Branch: "So why don't you do it anymore?"

Bernadette Fox: "I don't really know. I think I just lost that part of myself."

Bee Branch: "Maybe you didn't lose it. Maybe you just haven't used it in a while."

Elgie Branch: "You can't keep shutting everyone out."

Bernadette Fox: "I'm not shutting anyone out. I'm trying to survive Seattle."

Elgie Branch: "That's exactly what I'm talking about."

Bernadette Fox: "You married me knowing I wasn't exactly a people person."

Elgie Branch: "This is more than that, Bernadette."

Bee Branch: "You promised me Antarctica."

Bernadette Fox: "I know what I promised."

Bee Branch: "Then we're going."

Bernadette Fox: "You make it sound incredibly simple."

Bee Branch: "It is simple. You just have to actually go."
