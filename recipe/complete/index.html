A little about these muffins

These cottage cheese protein muffins are a pretty easy breakfast to make. Everything goes into a blender and then into the oven. You can also add berries or chocolate chips if you want.

Ingredients

  • 3 eggs
  • 3/4 cup low-fat cottage cheese
  • 1 1/4 cups almond flour
  • 1/2 cup protein powder
  • 1/4 cup sweetener (your choice)
  • 2 tbsp maple syrup
  • 1/2 tbsp baking powder
  • 1/2 tbsp lemon juice
  • 2 tsp vanilla extract
  • 1/2 tsp sea salt
  • 1/3 cup coconut or avocado oil

Instructions

  1. Preheat oven to 350°F and grease a muffin tin.
  2. Add all ingredients to a blender or food processor. Blend until there aren't any chunks left. Fold in berries or other add-ins after blending. If using frozen berries, chop them into small pieces.
  3. Divide the batter evenly into the muffin tin. It should make around 8 muffins.
  4. Bake for 20-25 minutes. The tops should feel firm and a toothpick should come out clean. Frozen berries might mean a few more minutes in the oven.
  5. Let them cool a little and enjoy! Store them in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

some extra notes

Don't overbake them or they can get dry. Berries are a good addition, but frozen ones can make the middle more moist. You can warm them up again before eating.
