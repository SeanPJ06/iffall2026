Meet the Busiest Man

Career

Richard Scarry was an author and illustrator whose picture books invite readers into a busy, friendly world. His stories turn ordinary places into scenes full of things to notice, name, and explore.

In Busytown, animals run shops, deliver mail, build houses, and go about their day. Characters such as Huckle Cat and Lowly Worm make these everyday adventures feel wonderfully familiar.

A world of little details

Part of the fun is looking closely. A tiny vehicle, a funny expression, or an unexpected character can turn a single page into a story of its own.

His books encourage curiosity about other people and the work they do. There is always another corner of Busytown to discover.
